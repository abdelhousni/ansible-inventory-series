== 1. --limit applies to every play
  hosts: db, --limit app: none, and ad hoc ansible exits 1
  hosts: app, --limit app: app1 app2 stg-app1

$ ansible-playbook site.yml
PLAY [Configure the databases]
    db play on db1 stg-db1
PLAY [Deploy the application, two hosts at a time]
    app1 in batch app1 app2
    app2 in batch app1 app2
    run_once ran on app1
PLAY [Deploy the application, two hosts at a time]
    stg-app1 in batch stg-app1
    run_once ran on stg-app1

$ ansible-playbook site.yml --limit app
PLAY [Configure the databases]
skipping: no hosts matched
PLAY [Deploy the application, two hosts at a time]
    app1 in batch app1 app2
    app2 in batch app1 app2
    run_once ran on app1
PLAY [Deploy the application, two hosts at a time]
    stg-app1 in batch stg-app1
    run_once ran on stg-app1

== 2. run_once runs once per serial batch

$ ansible-playbook site.yml --limit app:!app2
PLAY [Configure the databases]
skipping: no hosts matched
PLAY [Deploy the application, two hosts at a time]
    app1 in batch app1 stg-app1
    stg-app1 in batch app1 stg-app1
    run_once ran on app1

== 3. Facts of hosts outside the limit

$ ansible-playbook facts.yml
PLAY [Gather facts on the databases]
PLAY [Read db1 from the app hosts]
    ansible_limit: not set
    groups.db: db1 stg-db1
    db1 env, an inventory variable: prod
    db1 system, a fact: Linux

$ ansible-playbook facts.yml --limit app
PLAY [Gather facts on the databases]
skipping: no hosts matched
PLAY [Read db1 from the app hosts]
    ansible_limit: app
    groups.db: db1 stg-db1
    db1 env, an inventory variable: prod
    db1 system, a fact: missing

== 4. Two ways to get them back
-- gather them from the play that needs them, delegate_facts: true

$ ansible-playbook delegate-facts.yml --limit app1
PLAY [Read db1 from the app hosts]
    db1 system, a fact: Linux
-- or keep facts from an earlier run in a fact cache

$ ansible-playbook facts.yml --limit app
PLAY [Gather facts on the databases]
skipping: no hosts matched
PLAY [Read db1 from the app hosts]
    ansible_limit: app
    groups.db: db1 stg-db1
    db1 env, an inventory variable: prod
    db1 system, a fact: Linux
